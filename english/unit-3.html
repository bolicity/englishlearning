Before travelling abroad, make sure your passport and power adapter are packed.
出国旅行前，请务必准备好护照和电源转换插头。
Wherever you go, respect local customs and habits.
无论你去哪里，都要尊重当地的风俗习惯。
We were all shocked by the breathtaking scenery on top of the mountain.
我们都被山顶美不胜收的景色所震撼。
The warm hospitality of the locals made this trip truly memorable.
当地人的热情好客使这次旅行变得格外难忘。

They offered us fresh fruit and local snacks with great enthusiasm.

📘 语法解析
状语从句与特殊句型 (Adverbial Clauses & Expressions)
1. wherever 引导地点状语从句: Wherever you go, I will be with you. 2. whatever 引导名词性从句/状语从句: Share whatever you have. 3. can't wait to do sth. (迫不及待做某事) 4. be worth doing sth. (值得做某事)
