💬 重点句型与经典例句 (6句)
1 Before travelling abroad, make sure your passport and power adapter are packed. 🔊
出国旅行前，请务必准备好护照和电源转换插头。
2 Wherever you go, respect local customs and habits. 🔊
无论你去哪里，都要尊重当地的风俗习惯。
3 We were all shocked by the breathtaking scenery on top of the mountain. 🔊
我们都被山顶美不胜收的景色所震撼。
4 The warm hospitality of the locals made this trip truly memorable. 🔊
当地人的热情好客使这次旅行变得格外难忘。
5 I can't wait to share my travel experience on my blog. 🔊
我迫不及待想在博客上分享我的旅行见闻。
6 They offered us fresh fruit and local snacks with great enthusiasm. 🔊
他们热情地为我们提供了新鲜水果和当地特色小吃。
