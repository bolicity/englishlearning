🗣️ 经典例句 12
They are located in the city centre.
它们坐落于市中心。
Tall buildings show our progress in technology and design.
摩天大楼展现了我们在科技与设计上的进步。
Every few years, the record for the highest building is broken.
每隔几年，最高建筑的纪录就会被刷新。
The Palace Museum started to be built in 1406 and was completed in 1420.
故宫博物院始建于1406年，竣工于1420年。
Yellow was a symbol of power, used only by the emperors.
黄色是权力的象征，过去仅供皇帝专享。
Ancient Chinese wooden structures can stand up to severe earthquakes without single nails.
中国古代木结构建筑不用一根钉子就能经受住强震。
The upturned blocks under roofs act like springs against earthquakes.
屋檐下翘起的斗拱在抗震时起到了弹簧般的缓冲作用。
Modern architect designs combine traditional features with eco-friendly concepts.
现代建筑师的设计将传统特色与环保理念完美结合。
Waste and rubbish are quickly cleared in this environmentally friendly building.
在这座环保型建筑中，废弃物和垃圾都能被快速清运处理。
Green buildings reduce carbon footprints by absorbing CO2 and recycling rainwater.
绿色建筑通过吸收二氧化碳和循环利用雨水来减少碳足迹。
The green curtain produces oxygen and absorbs carbon dioxide.
绿帘植物制造氧气并吸收二氧化碳。
Only three workers were needed to print the whole building's walls.
打印整座建筑的墙体仅仅需要三名工人。
📘 语法解析
形容词与分词短语 (Adjectives & Participle Modifiers)
1. 过去分词短语作后置定语: buildings built of steel (用钢铁建成的建筑) 2. 介词短语: without any nails (没有任何钉子) 3. 不可数名词与数词表达: thousands of years of Chinese culture
