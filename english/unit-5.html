💬 重点句型与经典例句 (12句)
1 They are located in the city centre. 🔊
它们坐落于市中心。
2 Tall buildings show our progress in technology and design. 🔊
摩天大楼展现了我们在科技与设计上的进步。
3 Every few years, the record for the highest building is broken. 🔊
每隔几年，最高建筑的纪录就会被刷新。
4 The Palace Museum started to be built in 1406 and was completed in 1420. 🔊
故宫博物院始建于1406年，竣工于1420年。
5 Yellow was a symbol of power, used only by the emperors. 🔊
黄色是权力的象征，过去仅供皇帝专享。
6 Ancient Chinese wooden structures can stand up to severe earthquakes without single nails. 🔊
中国古代木结构建筑不用一根钉子就能经受住强震。
7 The upturned blocks under roofs act like springs against earthquakes. 🔊
屋檐下翘起的斗拱在抗震时起到了弹簧般的缓冲作用。
8 Modern architect designs combine traditional features with eco-friendly concepts. 🔊
现代建筑师的设计将传统特色与环保理念完美结合。
9 Waste and rubbish are quickly cleared in this environmentally friendly building. 🔊
在这座环保型建筑中，废弃物和垃圾都能被快速清运处理。
10 Green buildings reduce carbon footprints by absorbing CO2 and recycling rainwater. 🔊
绿色建筑通过吸收二氧化碳和循环利用雨水来减少碳足迹。
11 The green curtain produces oxygen and absorbs carbon dioxide. 🔊
绿帘植物制造氧气并吸收二氧化碳。
12 Only three workers were needed to print the whole building's walls. 🔊
打印整座建筑的墙体仅仅需要三名工人。
