📘 语法解析
定语从句 (Relative Clauses: who / which / that)
1. 先行词为人时，引导词用 who 或 that: • The girl who helps me is my deskmate. • The man who restores old books is Mr. Du. • The boy that you saw is a book restorer. 2. 先行词为物时，引导词用 which 或 that: • This is the book which I bought last week. • The clay pot which is colourful is Mexican art. • I like songs that are soft. 3. 只能用 that 的情况 (包含人与物 / 最高级 / 序数词 / all, everything 等): • He talked about the teachers and schools that he visited. • This is the most valuable book that I have ever read. • Tell me everything that you know. 4. 介词后不能用 that，用 in which / for whom 等: • This is the house in which I lived.
