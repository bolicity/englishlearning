🗣️ 经典例句 10
Folk customs connect us back to our cultural roots.
民间习俗将我们与自己的文化根基紧密相连。
Setting off fireworks on New Year's Eve is believed to wash away bad luck.
除夕夜燃放烟花被认为能够洗去厄运。
Traditional festivals serve as a bridge between the past and the present.
传统节日构筑起了连接过去与现在的桥梁。
People wear colourful folk costumes and perform traditional dances during the festival.
节日期间，人们身穿绚丽的民族服饰，表演传统舞蹈。
Folk customs are the mirror of the past.
民俗文化是照见历史过去的一面镜子。
As people from different backgrounds learn about each other, society becomes stronger.
随着来自不同背景的人们相互了解，社会变得更加凝聚强大。
Traditional dishes are cooked with love and shared among family and friends.
传统美食饱含爱意被精心烹制，并在亲朋好友间共享。
An old man painting with sugar caught my eye in the street.
街头一位画糖人的老人吸引了我的目光。
Thousands of monkeys enjoy a big fruit feast every November in Thailand.
每年十一月，泰国成千上万只猴子享用着丰盛的水果盛宴。
This cultural treasure will disappear if we don't find ways to save it.
如果我们不想法设法挽救，这一文化瑰宝将会消逝。
