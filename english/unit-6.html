About eight million tons of plastic waste is thrown into the ocean every year.

It can take hundreds of years for plastic to break down naturally.
塑料自然分解可能需要数百年的时间。
Tiny pieces of plastic are harder to clean up and more harmful.

Its special lights make plastic stand out from sand and seaweed.

She failed many times but she never gave up testing the robot.

Everyone can make a difference to cut plastic pollution.
每一个人都能为减少塑料污染贡献一份力量。
The pollution problem will be solved little by little.
污染问题将会一点一点逐步得到解决。
Air pollution will not be reduced if we take no action.
如果我们不采取行动，空气污染将不会减少。
Broken objects won't be thrown away in the school repair centre.

All useful suggestions will be printed in the school newspaper.

Will more recycling bins be placed around our campus?

Plastic waste is harmful to both the environment and our health.

I recommend that the school use recycled plastic for art works.

Clear signs can be put up to guide correct waste sorting.

If the proposal could be carried out, plastic waste will drop greatly.

📘 语法解析
原因与结果状语及一般将来时被动语态 (Passive Voice in Future Tense)
1. 一般将来时的被动语态: Subject + will (not) be + past participle • The pollution problem will be solved little by little. • Air pollution will not be reduced if we take no action. 2. 条件状语从句主将从现: If the proposal is carried out, plastic waste will drop greatly.
