噪音通常是由风扇和空调引起的。

交通噪音是城市噪音的最大诱因。
3 Road works play a big part in noise pollution too. 🔊
道路施工也在噪音污染中占很大比重。
4 I couldn't agree more! 🔊
我完全赞同！
5 About eight million tons of plastic waste is thrown into the ocean every year. 🔊
每年大约有八百万吨塑料垃圾被倒入海洋。
6 It can take hundreds of years for plastic to break down naturally. 🔊
塑料自然分解可能需要数百年的时间。
7 Tiny pieces of plastic are harder to clean up and more harmful. 🔊
细小的微塑料碎片更难清理，危害也更大。
8 Its special lights make plastic stand out from sand and seaweed. 🔊
其特殊的照明光线使塑料能够从沙子和海藻中显现脱颖而出。
9 She failed many times but she never gave up testing the robot. 🔊
她失败了很多次，但她从未放弃对机器人的测试。
10 Everyone can make a difference to cut plastic pollution. 🔊
每一个人都能为减少塑料污染贡献一份力量。
11 The pollution problem will be solved little by little. 🔊
污染问题将会一点一点逐步得到解决。
12 Air pollution will not be reduced if we take no action. 🔊

18 Clear signs can be put up to guide correct waste sorting. 🔊
