💬 重点句型与经典例句 (6句)
1 Many teenagers seem to suffer from stress in their daily lives. 🔊
许多青少年在日常生活中似乎承受着压力。
2 When faced with peer pressure, we should learn how to calm strong emotions. 🔊
面对同龄人压力时，我们应该学会如何平复强烈的情绪。
3 Talking to a peer counsellor is a good way to ease your troubles. 🔊
与同龄辅导员倾诉是缓解烦恼的好方法。
4 If you have a hard time getting along with others, ask a teacher for advice. 🔊
如果你在与他人相处上有困难，可以向老师寻求建议。
5 His hard work finally paid off and he achieved his dream. 🔊
他的努力最终得到了回报，实现了梦想。
6 No matter what difficulties you face, always try to stay positive. 🔊
无论面对什么困难，都要尽量保持积极乐观。
