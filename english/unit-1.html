Many teenagers seem to suffer from stress in their daily lives.
许多青少年在日常生活中似乎承受着压力。
When faced with peer pressure, we should learn how to calm strong emotions.
面对同龄人压力时，我们应该学会如何平复强烈的情绪。
Talking to a peer counsellor is a good way to ease your troubles.
与同龄辅导员倾诉是缓解烦恼的好方法。
If you have a hard time getting along with others, ask a teacher for advice.
如果你在与他人相处上有困难，可以向老师寻求建议。

📘 语法解析
建议与表达 (Giving Advice)
1. You had better (not) do sth. (你最好做/不做某事) 2. Why not do sth. / Why don't you do sth.? (为什么不做某事呢？) 3. I advise you to do sth. (我建议你做某事)
